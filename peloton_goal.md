# High level goal
Build a local webapp (.html file) that mimic the walking tour of scenic city/historic town/trail/monument/national park/exoic places, like what Peloton threadmill displays.

# Detail
- The app include a button called "random video". Upon pressing the button, find a new random video like this one https://youtu.be/2BfHMnDOteA?si=zsfvBFIKzIfUF1Bv (which is one of the result from my search for "Walking tour" in youtube) and play it on the screen.
- Keep a running record of videos played so far (played list), and allow user to tag any particular video as a favorite (favorite list) while being played. Maintain both lists, and allow user to go to a list and display them in thumbnail/tile and pick one to play.
